Full cycle of a foreign trade transaction

From supplier qualification to cargo release in the country of destination.

01

Procurement and supplier qualification

Selecting the manufacturer to the buyer’s specification, verifying compliance with GOST / EN / ASTM, incoming inspection, checking certificates and quality passports.

Documents: specification, quality certificate (incl. 3.1 under EN 10204).

02

Contracting and currency control

Foreign trade contract on Incoterms 2020 terms (FCA, CPT/CIP, DAP), registration of the contract with the authorised bank and currency control support.

Documents: contract, specifications, proforma invoice, unique contract number (UCN).

03

Customs clearance and origin

HS classification under the EAEU Commodity Nomenclature, export declaration, EAV certificate of origin via the Chamber of Commerce, ISPM 15 fumigation of wooden packaging. Preparation of dangerous goods (classes 3, 8, etc.) under IATA DGR and the IMDG Code.

Documents: export declaration, invoice, packing list, EAV certificate, DGD, SDS.

04

Logistics

Air shipments, including dangerous goods, and sea container freight FCL/LCL. Cargo securing and tracking until release to the consignee; cargo insurance through our partner broker.

Documents: AWB/HAWB, bill of lading (B/L), insurance policy.
